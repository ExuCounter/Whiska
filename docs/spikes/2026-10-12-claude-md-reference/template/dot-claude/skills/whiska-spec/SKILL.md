---
name: whiska-spec
description: "Write the spec the person approves before any code. Use after grilling, or at once when there was nothing to ask."
---

Write `.whiska-spec.md` at the repo root: a title, "What changes" (3 to 6 bullets) and
"Testing" (which test proves it). Write no code. Then end the turn asking the person for
their ok on the spec, naming the file, with the decision marker (two U+2063 characters) as
the last line.
