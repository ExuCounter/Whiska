---
name: grilling
description: "Ask the person the costly choices before building. Use when a brief leaves a costly choice open."
---

Ask every costly question in one message: number each, letter its options, give a
recommended letter, and end with a reply line such as "reply 1A 2B". Ask nothing cheap.
Then end the turn; in a mouse, the decision marker (two U+2063 characters) is the last line.
