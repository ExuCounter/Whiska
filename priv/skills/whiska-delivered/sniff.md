# A finished investigation with a proposal

Two signs together: the message carries a **Proposed build** block with
Found, Build and Touches lines, and the branch has nothing to merge — the
heading `whiska show <id>` printed names it with `(sniff)` after it, a
mouse that could only look, or the line straight under the heading is
exactly `On the branch: nothing committed beyond <base> · nothing
uncommitted`. Then offer one AskUserQuestion holding these two options,
in this order:

- **Build what it proposes** — its preview is the block's Found, Build and
  Touches lines, verbatim, so the person decides on the proposal itself
  rather than on a label. Its description: a fresh session builds it,
  shaped for the build.
- **Not now** — send nothing and leave the mouse as it is. Say so in one line.

No "(Recommended)" on any of them, whatever `## Finish` names: the one thing
asked is whether the proposal is right, and only the person's read of it can
say. There is no Drop here: nobody has picked it on an investigation, and a branch
with nothing on it loses nothing by staying.

Text typed into "Other" is the person's notes or objections on the proposal. Send
it to the mouse as `finished.md`'s section "Text typed into Other" says. It is not
a pick: nothing is built.

On **Build what it proposes**, follow the `spawn-worktree` skill's section
"Building what an investigation proposed" with this question's id. Nothing
else is asked — not the branch name, the model or the effort: that section
derives each and shows them in one line.

No proposal, or neither sign of a branch with nothing to merge → read
`finished.md` beside this file instead: a plain reply from a mouse with
nothing on its branch gets no picker there.
